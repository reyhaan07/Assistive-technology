👁 Eye blink communication

For people who cannot move – communicate with blinks only. A caregiver sets the mode once; it is remembered.

Face: - Eyes: - Last blink: -
2 quick blinksYes
3 quick blinksNo
Long blink (~1 s)I need help
Very long blink (~2 s)Open the blink board

On the blink board, items light up one after another – a long blink selects the lit item. Space bar / click also selects (for testing or caregivers).
